Aeroplan books Star Alliance only — airBaltic & SAS are out

The mistake to avoid: thinking each hop is its own little award. It isn't. Aeroplan prices one one-way as a single award off the total distance of all its segments, then adds 5,000 points if — and only if — you sit somewhere 24 hours or more (that's a stopover; under 24h is free). So the game is: chain short hops, keep every layover under a day, and take exactly one deliberate stopover per ticket for a "fortress" work week.

the mechanic — corrected01
how it's priced

One award, summed distance

A one-way = one award, priced on the Within Europe economy band by the sum of every segment's distance — not per-hop. Chart changed Jun 1 2026, so treat every point figure here as ≈ verify live.

the 24-hour line

Layover free · stopover 5k

Connect in under 24h → free, still one ticket, and you still get to walk the city. Sit ≥24h → that's a stopover, +5,000 pts, max one per one-way. Use it on purpose for your work week.

correction · carriers

airBaltic is NOT Star Alliance

Gemini routed you RIX→VNO→TLL on airBaltic — Aeroplan can't book it (nor SAS, which left the alliance in 2024). Baltic hops on Star Alliance must bounce through a hub: LOT/Warsaw, LH/Frankfurt·Munich, Austrian/Vienna, Turkish/Istanbul.

correction · pricing

7,500 isn't the partner rate

The draft's "0–1,000 mi = 7,500" is Air Canada's own chart, not partner economy. Partner intra-Europe runs higher and by total journey distance — hub backtracking inflates the band fast (see §3).

booking reality

The engine won't build it — you will

aeroplan.com won't auto-assemble a 4-segment self-transfer. Build it multi-city, and if it balks, call the desk (1-833-247-6668) and dictate the segments. HUCA if the first agent won't. Stamp every hold in UTC.

the sharp move

Don't force the Baltics onto points

Forcing RIX·VNO·TLL through hubs triples the miles and needs backtracking. Buy those three as airBaltic cash micro-hops (~€30–50, <200 mi each) — each landing still counts as a new country. Save points for the long legs & the Balkans.
