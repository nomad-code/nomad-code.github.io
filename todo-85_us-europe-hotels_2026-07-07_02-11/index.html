Where you actually land

For a US + Europe traveler the market collapses to a clean choice. Both paths keep Hilton as the forever anchor and Marriott as the second chain — the only question is whether IHG earns a third slot.

recommended

Core + IHG

Hilton Aspire (forever) · Marriott Boundless now → Brilliant 2028 · IHG Premier. IHG's dense European footprint + a 40k cert with unlimited top-off + 4th-night-free is the best cert-value story for pricey Western cities. Three anniversary nights a year, two Chase slots.

Just Hilton + Marriott

Drop IHG too. Two chains that already blanket every US and European city you'll visit; the simplest possible wallet, one Chase slot. You give up IHG's European density and 4th-night-free — but you were never going to run out of Hilton or Marriott options in the West.

04 · the europe edgestatus pays at breakfast

In Europe, your status feeds you

The single biggest reason the two-chain core is better in Europe than the US: elite breakfast rules flip at the Atlantic. Where a €40 breakfast is normal, free breakfast for two is real money — every stay.

StatusSourceIn the USIn Europe
Hilton DiamondAspire, forever F&B credit only (~$10–25/pp) — no guaranteed breakfast Free full breakfast for two. The rule still holds in 2026 — your forever card earns most where you travel most.
Marriott PlatinumBrilliant (2028) Continental breakfast (or 750 pts) when no lounge Full breakfast, no continental restriction. Europe is where Platinum stops being a paper tier.
IHG PlatinumIHG Premier No breakfast — upgrades/points only No breakfast either. IHG's value is footprint + certs + 4th-night-free, never the status perks.
Hyatt Discoverist— cut No breakfast; 2pm checkout No breakfast; thin European footprint. The reason Hyatt drops out.
Net effect: the two statuses you actually hold under this plan — Hilton Diamond now, Marriott Platinum from 2028 — both convert to free full breakfast the moment you land in Europe. That's a per-stay benefit the fee math below doesn't even count.

06 · trapsthe ways this goes wrong

What still bites

do

  • Boundless before everything — it starts the only 24-month clock in the plan
  • Lean on IHG for Europe, Marriott + Hilton for blanket coverage everywhere
  • Book the Hilton uncapped cert at a European Waldorf/Conrad — highest-value single night in the deck
  • Use IHG Premier Business if the freed 5/24 slot is tighter than assumed
  • Calendar the Brilliant unlock: Boundless bonus-post + 25 months
  • Take Aspire by 7/29 — the one hard deadline

don't

  • Keep the Hyatt card out of habit — for US/Europe it's the weakest fee in the deck
  • Chase Wyndham/Choice for Western cities — economy-tier, non-central
  • Touch any Marriott card besides Boundless → Brilliant — they share one 24-month bonus budget
  • Assume Diamond gets you US breakfast — it's a credit stateside, breakfast only abroad
  • Miss a minimum spend — approvals recover, a burned SUB doesn't
