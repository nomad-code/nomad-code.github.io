reading the hole

Each trace is one shot: where it started, its carry apex, where it finished. Color is the strokes-gained score — the same per-shot number your Spica already computes. Filter to a stratum and the pattern jumps out: the approach misses cluster short-right, exactly where the flick + weak grip send the face. That's the leak, as a place on the map — and it points at the same fix the swing lab does.

the real thing · when you want it02 / the stack

GSPro courses × Esri 3D × strokes-gained shot traces.

This page is the self-contained prototype. The full build ties the traces to real geography — TECH-481 · esri-3d, but with your shots and your strokes gained on it. The data already exists: Spica logs, the ArcGIS 3D APIs, and GSPro's laser-scanned course geometry. Here's the stack.

1

Terrain

Esri 3D world elevation + imagery for the real course (Pebble, Mammoth Dunes) — the ground truth GSPro already scanned.

2

Shot traces

Each Spica shot — start x,y → carry → finish — drawn as an arc on the terrain. Every round, layered.

3

Strokes-gained heat

Color each trace by its SG score. Red where you bled, green where you gained. The leak becomes a place.

4

Stratum overlay

Filter to tee / approach / around-green / putting and watch the misses cluster spatially, not just in a table.

Feasibility: real ArcGIS integration renders 3D via a SceneView with world-elevation ground and elevated polyline graphics — the one place the build brings in an external SDK. The loop works today with zero of it built; the Esri map is the surface you earn once the log has rounds in it. It belongs in bpo — personal.
