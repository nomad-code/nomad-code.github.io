Grade your surf.
Medewi + Yeh Sumbul.

Flowstate is your instant replay + AI tag engine — it films every wave, fingerprints you out of the pack, and tags your snaps, cutbacks and barrels before you're out of your wetsuit. What it doesn't hand you is a grade. This is the rubric that turns its raw tags into a score you can chase — session over session, spot over spot — so progress stops being a feeling and becomes a number.

your session ↗ Tim's session ↗
01 · dial it in

Mobile Capture — the ocean move

no fixed cam · you film

Flowstate's tower cameras live at surf parks and wave pools — there are none at Medewi or Yeh Sumbul. At an outdoor break you run Mobile Capture: turn an iPhone (iOS 16.6+) into the cam yourself. The AI does the rest — it tracks and follows you inside a static frame, so no wristband, no board sticker, no panning.

The set-up 2 min

  • 1Mount high & still. Tripod on the beach, a wall, or a lifeguard/warung roof. The cam doesn't pan — give it the whole peak.
  • 2Frame takeoff → kickout. Fit the full ride in shot; on a point that means aiming down the line, not straight out.
  • 3Sun at its back. Backlit water blows out the tags — keep the sun behind the lens.
  • 4Roll before you paddle. Start the session recording, then go surf. Body-fingerprint AI finds you in the crowd after.

What comes back

  • ✓Your waves, auto-clipped. The model groups every wave that's yours into one session — watchable by the time you're changed.
  • ✓Move tags. Snaps, cutbacks, floaters, barrels — labelled on the clip, so you see which moves you actually landed and when.
  • ✓Stats. Wave count, ride length, frequency — and turn angles / session overlays / side-by-side on Pro.
  • ✓An auto highlight reel + a remote-coach marketplace if you want a real read on a session.
Free = capture · tags · basic stats  ·  Pro = turn angles · overlays · adaptive goals · side-by-side
02 · your two spots

Where to point the lens

west bali base

Two mellow West Bali waves, ten minutes apart — the perfect progression lab. Long, soft and repeatable means high wave count and lots of reps, which is exactly what a grading baseline needs.

Medewi Point the point

Soft, boulder-bottom left point that rolls 800m+ on its day — one of Bali's longest, and mellow enough to drill turns instead of surviving the drop. The ride length is the gift: you can stack three, four cutbacks on a single wave.

Film from the point / Bombora vantage, looking down the line · Grade for: turns-per-wave, wave count, linking.

Yeh Sumbul the beach

The sand beside Medewi — your stay base. More forgiving peaks and shorter, punchier sections. Less about the marathon ride, more about reps: clean takeoffs, first-turn commitment, wave after wave.

Film from the beach, framing the peak · Grade for: takeoff make-rate, first move, frequency.

Same rig, same rubric travels anywhere — point it at Lakey Peak or Scar Reef in Sumbawa and only the spot card changes.

05 · look doper

The part you actually feel

reel · comp · one move

Style is a score, not a vibe

The look-doper factor lives in projection and completion — driving through turns and riding them out — far more than raw move count. Grade it (line 6), watch it back, and it stops being luck. Pull the auto highlight reel each session; the reel is the receipt.

One move a trip · you vs Tim

Pick one signature move per trip — say a Medewi drawn-out cutback — and drill only that until the grade climbs. Then run a private comp: your session vs Tim's, same rubric. Two links, one scoreboard, all the motivation you need.

the loop — capture (Mobile Capture) → tag (Flowstate AI) → grade (this rubric, top-3 mean) → log (dna/surf, SXX/<n>W) → one goal → repeat. Grades are yours; DONE is yours.
