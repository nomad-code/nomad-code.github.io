Nukeable by design

strava-nix thesis · account = deploy target, not truth

The archive on disk is the source of truth. Any Strava account is a disposable target — deploy rebuilds it from the archive, so it can be nuked and restored, identical, at will. Omar (omarali80361) is the sandbox that proves it.
