Sweep UR → Hyatt before oct 1

Satellites, if wanted 2027

Choice MC ($0, Japan Comfort redemptions) and/or Amex Marriott Business ($125, extra 35k cert) once the dust settles. Neither is time-sensitive; elevated offers on both recur.

Marriott Brilliant ~sep 2028

24 months + buffer after the Boundless bonus posts. Platinum + 25 elite nights + 85k cert. Set the reminder at Boundless bonus-post date + 25 months. Until then, Boundless certs carry the Marriott wing.

The real constraint isn't approvals — it's minimum spend. Boundless $3k + Hyatt $3k + Aspire $6k + Citi's SUB + IHG $3k ≈ $15–21k of required spend inside ~6 months, all overlapping. The sequence above staggers the clocks (each SUB window opens at approval), but if organic spend can't cover it, stretch steps 2 and 5 out rather than missing a bonus — a missed SUB is the only unrecoverable error in this plan.

06 · trapsthe ways this goes wrong

Sequencing traps, named

do

  • Boundless before everything — it starts the only 24-month clock in the plan
  • Both Chase apps in July, 7–14 days apart (2-per-30 is the ceiling)
  • Aspire by 7/29; Amex and Chase velocity don't see each other same-month
  • Use IHG Premier Business if the 5/24 math turns out tighter than assumed
  • Calendar the Brilliant unlock: Boundless bonus post-date + 25 months
  • Keep every card's cert redeemed before its next fee posts — the fee is the cert's price

don't

  • Apply for any non-plan personal card before October — every slot is budgeted
  • Touch Amex Marriott Business before Boundless — its bonus kills the Boundless bonus for 24 months
  • Touch Bevy, ever — and note Brilliant permanently blocks Bevy's bonus (fine; Bevy isn't wanted)
  • Chase Wyndham for Caesars — that door closed Feb 2025
  • Miss a minimum spend — approvals are recoverable, a burned SUB is not
  • Cancel the Hyatt card in a weak year — no downgrade path exists; it's keep-or-lose-forever
What stays true from v1: Venture X, Ink Preferred, and Savor keep their jobs; Sapphire still downgrades at next fee; Platinum and United Club stay skipped. This build adds the hotel wing around that core — total fleet fees land at $1,924/yr in 2026 (v1's $1,635 + $289 for the Chase trio), against a credit-and-cert stack that clears it before a single point is spent.
