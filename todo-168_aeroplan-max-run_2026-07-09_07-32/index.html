the container

12 segments · 6 directions

One reservation holds up to 6 directions of travel, ≤6 segments each, 12 segments total, and one stopover per direction (+5k, ≥24h). A <24h connection is free and stays on the same direction — that's how a direction grazes several capitals.

how it's priced

One direction, summed distance

Each direction = one partner award priced on the Within Europe band by the sum of its segments (empirically ≤~800mi ≈ 7.5k · 1,001–2,000mi ≈ 12.5k · longer ≈ 15k). Chart shifted Jun 1 2026 — every figure here is ≈ verify live.

the strategy — three tiers03

Maximizing countries isn't "book everything on points." It's matching each leg to its cheapest tool. Three tiers:

tier 1 · points

The long jumps

Transatlantic in/out, the Baltic sweep (airBaltic 7.5k hops), the Baltic→Balkan jump, and the island annex (Malta / Cyprus). Where distance is real, points win.

tier 2 · overland

The Balkan spine

9 countries by FlixBus/train, ~€10–25 a hop, one night each. The densest new-country-per-euro stretch on earth — never fly it.

tier 3 · cash flights

The awkward gaps

Moldova (KIV) and the islands when points space is dry — Wizz/Ryanair via Kiwi/Skyscanner, often €20–60. Cheaper than forcing a hub-bounce award.
