Two tiers

cheap vs. the real labor
Tier 1 · mechanical

The manifest

timeline-manifest walks a place folder → a CSV of stay · category · utc · name · ext · bytes. The note's skeleton — dates, path, N files · MB · category counts — is regenerable from it. Read-only, idempotent, stdlib.

Tier 2 · enrichment

Opening the files

Docs named, people-card names, WhatsApp counterparties, webloc subjects — none of it lives in the CSV. Spots merge Google Maps saved-list exports (found sitting in the archives) with Address-Book place tags.

The living saved-list

the Linear model, settled
two layers + a rule

Relevance flows up

Active — one live project = the place you're in now. Archive — past stays are Completed, documented by the note + CSV. Promotion — a curation tier moves what you loved into the active project when you return. Like a Maps saved list: keep, drop, or star.

curation tier
👎 dropnot recorded
👍 keepstays in the note roster
⭐ prioritypropagates forward · seeds the active project

Stars are earned, not invented — a loved-doc, or recurrence across ≥2 stays.
