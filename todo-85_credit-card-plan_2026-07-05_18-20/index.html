05 · the VA questionmail ≠ domicile

Ship the cards to Toano. It's fine.

A mailing address is not a domicile factor Virginia's regulations enumerate — residency turns on license, voter/vehicle registration, banking, and 183+ days of actual presence or abode (Va. Code § 58.1-302; 23VAC10-110-30). Both addresses already live on the credit profile; cards arriving at the parents' house adds one weak, already-existing data point. One structural fix makes it clean:

The one move that matters: at every issuer, set residential / KYC address = Texas and mailing address = Toano. Every major issuer supports the split. Bureau records and bank files stay anchored to TX; plastic ships to VA.

keep doing

  • TX driver's license — renew from abroad if needed
  • TX voter registration — overseas ballots can't be held against domicile (UOCAVA)
  • Vehicle reg, bank home branch, estate docs → all Texas
  • Stay under 183 days of VA presence per tax year
  • Keep the evidence file: flight records, foreign leases, TX ties

never do

  • Get a VA license or register a car in VA — the strong indicators
  • File a VA resident return "just in case" — that's affirmative evidence
  • List Toano as residential on W-9s or brokerage accounts — 1099 feeds trigger VA match letters
  • VA voter registration, club or gym memberships

General information, not tax advice — worth one cheap consult with a state-tax CPA. Virginia is a known sticky state, but that machinery targets ex-VA residents claiming they left; a TX domiciliary receiving mail at a family address is a far weaker fact pattern. "Can always change later" — correct, and nothing here is hard to unwind.
