What the pipeline protects

  • Read-only delegated access to Brendan’s own chats.
  • One named person or exact chat ID per sync.
  • Refresh token in macOS Keychain, never Git.
  • Raw messages under git-ignored .private/.
  • Teams text blockquoted and marked as untrusted input.

What it deliberately does not do

  • No tenant-wide Chat.Read.All crawl.
  • No Teams plugin, MCP, bot, or hidden connector.
  • No full-history import without --all-history.
  • No automatic promotion of raw chat into GitHub.
  • No claim that live ingestion succeeded before consent.
