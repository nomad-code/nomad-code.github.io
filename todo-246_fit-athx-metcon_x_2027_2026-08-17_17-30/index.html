What to train, in priority order

  1. Sandbag, the real thing. Get a 70 KG bag. It does not transfer from a barbell — the shape and the front-rack hold are the exercise. Squats in 5s–10s, carries 60 m unbroken.
  2. DB jerk at 22.5 dual. EMOM 5–8 reps for ten minutes. It doubles as shoulder-to-overhead practice for the Strength workout — the two share a pattern this year.
  3. DB bench volume. 60 reps at 22.5 dual is grip and shoulder endurance. 5 × 15 short rest, then add fatigue before it.
  4. Burpees over bench. Cheapest station on the sheet — drill 45 unbroken at 17/min.
  5. Row calories after fatigue. Never fresh. The second 60 is the one that hurts: 5 × 20 cal after lifting.
