Fully automated round sync

Play the round, record the swings — the nightly refresh (launchd com.bpo.golf-refresh, 22:07) now finds new SGT scorecards itself, runs the whole TODO-186/187 chain, publishes the round artifact, and pings a macOS notification with the URL. If discovery can't find a scorecard for a round you clearly played, it pings once asking for the URL instead of staying silent.

The nightly chain

sn_pull→ tag ingest→ video mirror→ sgt_discover→ registry re-pull→ strokes gained→ per-shot join→ unsynced check→ reports→ artifact→ deploy + ping

What was built

piecewhat it does
sgt_discover.py Logs into simulatorgolftour.com — the login is a plain csrf+POST (uname/pwd, no captcha, remember-me cookie) — keeps the session in ~/ark/golf/sgt/session.cookies, sweeps the quest dashboard + profile round tables + one level of data-load/detail-url fragments, and prints any scorecard/(tid)/(rid) pair not already in the registry.
discover step in refresh.py Feeds discovered pairs straight into the registry pull. Exit 3 (no credentials) skips quietly; exit 2 (login failed) pings an action notification; the rest of the chain always continues.
unsynced check Every GSPro roundId in SwingNerds courseData (≥8 shots) must be covered by a joined_*.json match. A round with no SGT join pings once — "paste the scorecard URL" — de-duped via unsynced_seen.json. Caught the known gap immediately: Portrush_GM_GSP #58, 114 shots, no scorecard pulled.
deploy + ping When new data renders an artifact, refresh.py runs scripts/artifact-deploy.sh (commit → push → Pages) and the notification carries the public URL. Priority: failure > action-needed > new-data+URL.

One-time setup (the only manual step left)

cat > ~/ark/golf/sgt/credentials.json <<'EOF'
{"email": "YOUR-SGT-EMAIL", "password": "YOUR-SGT-PASSWORD"}
EOF
chmod 600 ~/ark/golf/sgt/credentials.json

Until this exists the discover step skips and the unsynced check is the safety net — you still get pinged when a round needs its URL.

Honest edges

Quest-mode rounds show zero on the public profile tables, so discovery leans on the logged-in quest dashboard — its scorecard-link shape is unvalidated until the next round posts with credentials in place. If it misses, the unsynced ping is the guaranteed fallback, so the loop can't silently drop a round. SwingNerds JWT expiry still needs one app-open (existing notification covers it). M5/tailscale migration deliberately deferred — the brief says M3 first.
