The BPO blockchain is three small primitives

how can I trust git when you can force push? · how do I know that action was ME in the moment?

Git is already a Merkle DAG — every commit hashes its tree and its parents, so history content can't be silently altered. What force-push moves is a ref: a mutable pointer. The gap is ref-level, not object-level, and closing it doesn't take a blockchain. A blockchain's actual invention is consensus among mutually distrusting writers — bpo has exactly one writer. What it needs are the three guarantees a blockchain bundles, unbundled:

01

Three questions, three guarantees

⛓

Hash chain — integrity

"how can I trust git when you can force push?"

provenance/chain.jsonl: one hash-linked entry per record change. A rewritten history breaks prev linkage or digest assertions, and CI goes red on the very next push.

✍

Signatures — authenticity

"how do I know that action was ME in the moment?"

ssh-keygen -Y sign, namespace bpo-provenance, key resident on the machine that acted (m3/m5). A signed entry pins its entire ancestry — its hash covers prev transitively.

⚓

Anchor — existed by time T

"maybe the only way… is to build a BPO blockchain?"

Phase 2 route: head prints the tip hash; a raw curl to OpenTimestamps anchors it into Bitcoin. Piggyback a blockchain — never run one.

03

Honest guarantee — v1 claims no more than it holds

  • Accidental / casual rewrite: caught by CI on the next push. This is the everyday threat, and it is fully closed.
  • Signed spans: one laptop-signed entry near the tip protects everything before it — the signature covers prev transitively, back to genesis.
  • Resumed sessions: records legitimately mutate (the builder merges by id); verify honors the latest chain assertion, so real life stays green.
What v1 does not stop

You, with your own keys, force-pushing a fully rebuilt unsigned chain. CI alone can't catch that — laptops notice a shrunk chain on pull, and the phase-2 anchor closes it definitively: a hash timestamped into Bitcoin can't be un-happened, even by you.

To arm the signatures

On m3 and m5, paste each ~/.ssh/id_ed25519.pub into provenance/keys/allowed_signers (format is commented in the file). Until then entries are unsigned and verify warns — never fails.

04

The life-provenance road

The Apple-Watch-action-button question — HIIT 25, thrusters 2×15, was that me, then? — is the same schema. The chain doesn't care that today's records are Claude sessions: anything that lands as a JSON edge-record in provenance/ gets chained, signed, and (phase 2) anchored the moment it's written. Watch workouts, takeout-style activity exports, check-ins — each is one small ingest script away from the same three guarantees. The primitive is built; the feeds are routes.
