The field you're walking into

Round-by-round percentiles from the latest full pull (2026 format). p90 = the score that beats 90% of the field — the working target band. Caveat: ATHX has changed the round programme every year, so expect a 2027 remix — the field's engine stays the same even when the tests move.

Points pay consistency

Points = sum of your three workout ranks, lowest wins. A win and two mid-packs loses to three top-fives. Train the weakest workout first: cutting a 20-rank leak beats adding 10 kg to a strength you already podium.

Where fields are thin

Smaller cohorts (age bands, early-season stops) price ranks cheaper — same absolute score, better points. The per-event tables in the warehouse show which stops run shallow.

Doubles is a different game

Team scores sum both partners; the team-individual table shows exactly what each half of every podium pair lifted and covered. Split the rounds with Chris against that, not against gut feel.
