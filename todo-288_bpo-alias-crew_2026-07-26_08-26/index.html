Design laws
1
Soul vs throne
An alias is its repo + vault memberships + accounts; hardware is a throne it visits. Nuke-and-repopulate stays boring.
2
Credentials tied to jobs, not all jobs
One vault per job; an alias joins only the vaults of jobs it runs. Never private, banking, or company vaults.
3
One king at a time
Coronation = eSIM toggle + vault swap when Brendan changes countries. Auditable, reversible.
4
Souls never touch company infra
Persona identities live on personally-billed surfaces only; patterns may graduate, souls don't.
