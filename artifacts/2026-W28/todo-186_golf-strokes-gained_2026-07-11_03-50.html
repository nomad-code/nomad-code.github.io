How the data got here

SGT’s scorecard is a JavaScript app that shows scores but hides the shot data. Capturing the page’s network traffic surfaced three public, unauthenticated endpoints — the shot fragment carries every lie and distance-to-hole:

/scorecard/…/indv· /indv/stats· /indv/shots ← positions

From positions, strokes gained falls out of one identity: per-shot SG telescopes, so each hole’s total is exactly E(tee, length) − score. That holds even when SGT drops a stroke — penalties surface as a labelled residual (the Blue Ridge round had 9), and the engine asserts the identity on every hole.

Method honesty. Baseline: Broadie 2011 (Interfaces Table 9) + Putts Gained, PGA Tour ShotLink. Putting, approach and around-green use measured distances. Off-the-tee & round totals are optimistic upper bounds — SGT gives no hole yardage, so tee-to-pin length is reconstructed as travelled + remaining (≥ true length on offline drives). SGT’s own putt counts are unreliable and were re-derived from shot positions.
