How it joins

Instead, within each hole an order-preserving alignment pairs every SwingNerds read’s post-shot distance-to-pin against each SGT line’s end distance. The unit each GSPro relay used (meters vs yards — it varies by round) is not trusted; both hypotheses are scored per segment and geometry decides. This round resolved to meters. Phantom monitor rows (float-exact duplicate reads marking gimmies and penalties) carry inherited metrics and are dimmed below.
