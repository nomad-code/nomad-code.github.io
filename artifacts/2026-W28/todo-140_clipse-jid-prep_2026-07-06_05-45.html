03the ask · other ideas

Prep & day-of ideas

Red Rocks is a mountain venue, not a downtown club. The night is logistics as much as music — most of these are about the altitude, the cold, and the walk.

⛰Respect the altitude

6,450 ft, and the venue is a stair climb from the lots. Hydrate hard the day before, go easy on alcohol, and don't sprint the ramp. Ties into the existing altitude/hyrox notes.

🧥Layer for a 30° swing

November sun is warm at load-in and gone by headliner. Pack a real jacket, gloves, and a beanie — the stone benches leach heat. A packable blanket is not overkill.

🪑GA is stone benches

Seating is numbered rows on backless rock. Bring a stadium cushion or a rentable seat-back — three-plus hours on granite is the classic Red Rocks rookie mistake.

👜Clear-bag rules

Small clear bag / clutch policy. Check the current list, pack light, and you skip the long security line — get in for the opener instead of missing it.

🚗Arrive early, plan the exit

Upper south lot fills first and the exit is a single-road crawl. Either get there ~5:30 or take a rideshare to the drop lot — decide before, not at 11 PM in the cold.

🎧Album homework

Run Clipse's Let God Sort Em Out and J.I.D's God Does Like Paradise a few times first, then the classics (Hell Hath No Fury, The Never Story) so every drop lands.
