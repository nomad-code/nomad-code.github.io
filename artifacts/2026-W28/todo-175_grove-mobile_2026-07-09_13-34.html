the grove design system · mobile pass

grove, on your phone.

Artifacts were authored on a fixed 1440px canvas and shrunk to fit with zoom. On an iPhone that floored at 0.45 — a 648px comp jammed into a 390px screen. It looked terrible. Now, below 640px, grove stops shrinking and reflows: one readable column, right-sized type, nothing clipped. The fix lives in components.css, which every artifact already links — so the whole gallery, present and future, inherits it.

what broke

The zoom canvas couldn't shrink far enough

01

grove comps are drawn at a fixed 1440px width and scaled to the window with a single zoom value. That's elegant on a laptop. On a phone the maths run out of room.

the floor

zoom clamped at 0.45

Fitting 1440px into 390px needs a zoom of ~0.27. The clamp floor was 0.45, so the comp rendered 648px wide — 258px wider than the screen.

no phone tier

reflow stopped at 760px

The only responsive layer targeted tablets. Fixed-canvas artifacts got no reflow at all — they shrank-and-overflowed instead.

giant type

128px display numbers

Every size was a fixed pixel value. A 128px hero number on a 390px screen overran its own container.

the fix

Below 640px: reflow, don't shrink

02
canvas

Drop the zoom, go fluid

The fixed frame becomes width:100% and zoom:1. Content lays out at its true, readable size and stacks into one column.

the crux

min-width:0 on every child

Grid & flex items default to min-width:auto, so a wide table forces its whole column off-screen. Zeroing it lets wide content scroll inside its own box instead of the page.

grids

Everything to one column

Hero, KPIs, cards, bars, formula rows — plus a [class*="grid"] net that catches bespoke per-artifact layouts too.

type

Fluid display sizes

The oversized numbers and headings switch to clamp() on mobile only — desktop is byte-for-byte unchanged.

@media (max-width:640px){ .frame{width:100%!important} .frame:not(.overlay),.fit{zoom:1!important} /* reflow, not shrink */ .hero,.rails,.kpis{grid-template-columns:1fr!important} .frame *,.layout *{min-width:0} /* the rule that matters most */ .tw,.rb-wrap,[class*="wrap"]{overflow-x:auto} /* wide tables scroll */ .bignum .n{font-size:clamp(52px,18vw,96px)} }

the contract

Never bad on mobile again

04

What grove now guarantees for free, and the one thing a bespoke <style> block still owes.

grove handles automatically

  • ✓The zoom canvas reflows to one column below 640px — any .frame/.fit/.layout artifact.
  • ✓Every grove grid collapses; display type rescales; the topbar de-chromes.
  • ✓min-width:0 + overflow-wrap keep wide tables and long strings from overflowing.
  • ✓A [class*="grid"] net collapses most bespoke grids without you lifting a finger.

you still owe (bespoke layouts)

  • ›Prefer grove primitives over one-off grids — they're already mobile-safe.
  • ›If you hand-roll a multi-column grid, give it @media(max-width:640px){…1fr}.
  • ›Wrap wide tables in an overflow-x:auto box so they scroll, not the page.
  • ›Test at 390px before you ship — the fix is a floor, not a guarantee against everything.
