Both polished options keep your swings. The only watch data that ever joins the vault is data you capture.

roundabout · course-only

Great display, zero record

Swing detection needs a started round on a mapped course, dedupes same-spot swings (a range bay is one spot), HealthKit gets workout-level data only, no export. Lovely live lat/long on course; a dead end as an owned record.

golfshot swing id

Best range logger, same wall

On-Range mode logs tempo, hand speed, path, impact moment with near-zero false positives — the best off-the-shelf range logger found. And still: no export found.

200 Hz CoreMotion → vault

watchOS ships a 200 Hz accel+gyro API (CMBatchedSensorManager, Series 8+); Apple's SwingWatch sample is the whole pattern — detect, batch the window, timestamp, POST. Adds lat/long+time per swing → on-course strokes gained goes self-sufficient too.
