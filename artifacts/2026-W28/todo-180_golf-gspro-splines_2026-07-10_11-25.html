01 · what fell

The "join the Discord" checklist was ~90% myth

The official OPCD documentation is a public Google Doc — JS-walled in a browser, wide open via export?format=txt (386 KB, rescued to ark). Inside it: direct public Drive links to the toolset zip (Blender add-ons + OPCD Palette.gpl), QGIS support files, and the process PDF. theperfectlie.net (Feb 2026) publishes the version pins: Unity 2018.4.36f1 (mandatory), Blender 4.0.2, Inkscape 1.2+, and the full splining layer-name spec.

still gated GreenKeeper V4 (Windows packaging tool, stage 7) is the one genuinely registration-gated binary left — plus a Clender account if the cloud route is chosen, and any post-2021 palette revisions. That's the entire surviving human dependency on the OPCD community.

The conversion tool reads each imported curve's material color, converts linear→sRGB, and string-matches the hex; anything off-palette lands in a ColorError bucket. It also only touches objects whose names contain path — which Blender takes from the SVG element id. Both conventions are now baked into make_splines.py.

Miscellaneous——empty, spec conformance

Per-layer ST_Union makes "no overlapping same-color shapes" true by construction; rings are force-closed (Overpass leaves gaps GEOS rejects); water and cart paths clip to the course perimeter. Doc units are mm, numerically equal to terrain meters (width="4096mm") — the tool scales ×1000 on import. Two outputs: splines_work.svg (ortho embedded, for the human Inkscape pass) and splines_clean.svg (313 KB, the machine input, below).

splines_clean.svg — map-north, straight out of make_splines.py. QA: layer set, closed paths, palette fills, bounds, zero same-color overlaps, counts 18/48/19+fringe.
The same course as OPCD meshes — Blender 4.0.2 workbench render of postCUT.blend after the tool's Convert Materials & Cut ran headless. 180° vs map-north by the tool's mirror convention (matches Unity terrain; verify at S5, don't "fix").

05 · terrain call (H4 closed)

One 4096 m square @ 4097 px — the export we already have

OPCD's inner/outer split (inner ≤ ~1×2 km) doesn't fit a 3.35 km east-west Fazio routing: any inner crop that covers the course rounds straight back to a ~4 km square — which is exactly the QA'd S1 export (tworivers_4097.raw, 1 m/px, UInt16, all Unity import params in heightmap_meta.json). The 2049@2 m export stays as the perf fallback knob.
