Decisions that hold it together

  • Silero VAD is load-bearing — without it whisper loops on the silence in short memos.
  • No-backfill guard — first run just sets the watermark; it won't turn 256 old memos into tickets.
  • Idempotent — the dna note + memo-uuid marker mean re-runs are no-ops.
  • dna never ships — transcripts stay in the private repo, off the Pages allowlist.

One manual step

the only thing between here and hands-free

Grant Full Disk Access to the watcher

launchd jobs are sandboxed and can't read the Voice Memos container until granted FDA — until then watch.log says authorization denied. Add /bin/zsh and /usr/bin/python3 under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access, then launchctl kickstart -k gui/$(id -u)/com.bpo.voice-memos. Manual runs from a normal terminal already work — the grant is only for the always-on daemon.
