Funnelled into Strava

Strava is the Instagram of fitness · the description is the catch-all

Strava's API only holds activities — not steps, HRV, sleep or weight. So the full picture lives here (shareable page), and Strava is the funnel: missing workouts get backfilled, and each activity's description becomes the catch-all for the health context Strava can't model.

01 · parse
parse.py
stream every Apple Health export → deduped SQLite (all metrics, workouts, routes)
02 · rollup
rollup.py
distill to lifetime KPIs + monthly series → rollups.json
03 · picture
render.py
this page — the rich, publishable full picture
04 · backfill
backfill.py
diff Apple-Health workouts vs Strava → upload the gaps
05 · catch-all
enrich.py
append that-day health context into Strava descriptions
