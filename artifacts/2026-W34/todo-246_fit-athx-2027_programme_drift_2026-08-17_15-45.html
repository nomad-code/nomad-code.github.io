Targets that survive a scheme change

Estimated 1RM (Epley) is the only currency that transfers when the rep scheme moves — and the field proves it travels: the Pro Male squat median is 175 e1RM in 2025 (from a 5RM) and 168 in 2026 (from a 3RM), 4% apart across a full programme change.

The plan in year-agnostic terms

  1. Overhead press — certain to be tested, scheme unknown. Build across the 1–10 rep range, not to one number.
  2. Squat — very likely. Solid leaves it ~5% under field median; aggressive clears it.
  3. Hinge — even odds, and our strongest lift. Solid puts the deadlift exactly on the field median (210 e1RM).
  4. Weighted pull-up — likely in Pro, currently zero data. Standing target: +33 KG for 3. Cheapest insurance in the plan.
  5. Run always, then row and bike and ski. One rower-only winter is how you get caught by a bike year.
