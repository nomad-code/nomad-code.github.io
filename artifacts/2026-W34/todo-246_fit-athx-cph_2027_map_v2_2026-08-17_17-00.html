Three gains that cost no strength

Shoulder to overhead
+15 → 30%
It allows a dip and drive. On a 57 KG strict press a trained split jerk is 74–80 KG. Free KG — learn it in Phase 0.
The run buys ski metres
+227 m
Per minute off the 3 KM run, at 2:12/500 m ski pace. The run isn't scored — it's a bid for ski time, and running is our strength.
Deadlift is an average
2 × 1 min
Score is the mean of two 1-minute attempts. Bank lift one, push lift two. Pure rehearsal, no extra strength.

What changed in the plan, line by line

  1. Strict press → shoulder to overhead. Train the jerk from week one; it is the single cheapest score on the board.
  2. Row → ski erg. The rower stays only for MetCon X calories (60 + 60 at Pro). Ski becomes the primary erg — and we have never scored one.
  3. 3RM deadlift, averaged over two attempts. Rehearse the exact window protocol every Wednesday.
  4. Squat 3RM → 2RM. Heavier doubles, same progression.
  5. Pull-up insurance: cancelled. The published 2027 programme has no pull-up block — that risk is retired.
  6. MetCon X is knowable now. 22.5 KG dual DBs and a 70 KG sandbag. Train the bag itself; it does not transfer from a barbell.
