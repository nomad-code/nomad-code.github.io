Taste Map is the veteran: founded 2011 on a 10kg roaster, four Vilnius cafés, a rotating single-origin filter menu and a direct line to Daterra in Brazil. Domas Ivonis and Ieva Malijauskaitė hold national titles across Barista, Latte Art and Cup Tasters. The sensory room is the part that puts the education beyond the espresso bar. House pointer: the espresso tonic.

How it got here

pre-1940Brewed coffee — ibrik and moka — takes root harder than in Latvia or Estonia. Cafés become social institutions by the 19th century.
1940Soviet occupation severs the thread. Roasted coffee is a Moscow-rationed luxury; households fall back on chicory and barley.
1991Independence. German and Scandinavian equipment and ideas flood in; Italian espresso culture, seeded via late-Soviet machinery trade, is the first template.
2003 · 2005First national barista championship; SCA chapter established.
2007 · 2011Caffeine Roasters launches, becomes the largest chain across all three Baltics. Taste Map follows with a 10kg roaster.
2026Huracán named world's best roaster in El Salvador. Falstaff Coffee Guide Nordics evaluates 315 cafés across five countries and names Espresinė Best Roastery in Lithuania.

Eskedar CoffeePilies g. 26 + 3Eskedar Tilahun fled Ethiopia in 2005 — Sahara by jeep with 37 others over nine days, a Libyan prison, the Mediterranean in a small boat — and landed in a Lithuanian winter with a one-month-old. Learned the language, earned a Master's, built one of the city's most distinctive brands from nothing. African beans only, mostly Ethiopian, direct and small-batch, rooted in the buna ceremony. Her foundation is building a primary school in Yirgacheffe. She calls it diplomacy through coffee.

New arrivals

Both chose Vilnius as their first expansion outside their home country. That's the tell.

  • KALVE — the Latvian roaster, central and contemporary, paired with GRUDAS Bakery pastries.
  • Rocket Bean — Riga's celebrated roastery, Global Coffee Awards Europe Flat White Alternative gold, now present in the city.

Curators, not roasters

The connective tissue — specialty arrives without ceremony and tastes right.

  • Elska — shares space with the Pamėnkalnio Art Gallery; atmosphere and considered sourcing.
  • Karštos galvos — Pilies street, brunch menu with its own crowd.
  • Cuproom · DEMO — serious enough for anyone who knows what they're drinking.

Community & trends

Kavos Entuziastai — Asociacija „Kavos Entuziastai", the official association and the connective tissue: the Lithuanian Coffee Championships, education from water mineralogy to latte art, roastery excursions, and competitors sent abroad. Cup Tasters champion Miglė Lukoševičiūtė, national titleholder in 2020 and 2025, represented Lithuania in Geneva.

Note for planning: the Vilnius Coffee Festival, formerly Vilnius Coffee Days, is no longer running. Kavos Entuziastai absorbed the championship role.

  • Light roasts have crossed from enthusiast territory into the mainstream.
  • Origin and process transparency are genuine selling points now, not niche signifiers.
  • Alt milks standard · matcha gaining · cold brew has a loyal base.
  • Baltic distinction: hot espresso drinks — flat white, cappuccino, latte — still beat the heavily customised cold drinks dominating Western European and US menus.
  • Home espresso setups spreading, pulling demand for fresh, high-transparency beans.
