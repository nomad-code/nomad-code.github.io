Findings

Photo library frozen since 26 Aug needs you

The live library database has not changed since 2026-08-26 20:11 — four days — while Calendar synced minutes ago. Cyprus and Bulgaria appear in this ontology only because the calendar recorded them. Every frame shot since the 26th is missing from the corpus.

2024 recovered from calendar

2024 holds zero photos across both Apple and Google sources, but 2,994 calendar events. Reconstructed: Indonesia 163d, United States of America 75d, Philippines 33d, Japan 25d. A whole year of the ontology existed and was invisible to the photo pipeline.

14,814 redundant frames in 7,166 bursts

41% of the corpus sits inside a burst — frames shot within 20 seconds of each other, the same moment attempted repeatedly. Keeping one frame per burst would drop 14,814 frames without losing a single moment.

Border days resolve, transit days do not

Interpolation only fills a gap when the frames on both sides agree, so it never invents a crossing. That leaves flight days unresolved from photos alone — which is exactly where the calendar's timezone field takes over.
