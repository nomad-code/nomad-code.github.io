A guard without a remedy

The overnight photo job refused at 02:00. It was right to.

The log read only 13 GiB free (< 20) — refusing to export. That guard exists because a bare process.py filled this volume to 100% once already. It did its job. The defect is subtler: the loop has a refusal but no reclaim. Too full to export, and no stage whose job is to make room — so it stopped and waited for a human who did not know he was needed.

Two corrections

Nearly deleted the wrong thing

I had diffed all 88,775 filenames in the 48 GiB Takeout zip against the extracted directory beside it. Zero missing. I called it pure redundancy and moved to delete.

He stopped me: the zip is the form that works in Google Drive. Right call. It is uploading to gdrive-personal:zyx/photos/ instead, and the local copy stays until Drive confirms it. Verified-redundant is not the same as worthless.

Retracted a claim before he acted on it

I said the 133 GiB ~/Documents/My Drive could be freed by flipping Drive for Desktop from Mirror to Stream. Wrong. It is a plain Directory, not a DriveFS mount — the real mounts live at ~/Library/CloudStorage/GoogleDrive-*.

There is no setting to flip. Freeing that space means genuinely deleting local copies, which is a different decision than I had described. The mirror did not just stall — the workflow left DriveFS entirely.

Also proven along the way: rclone check against Drive found 9,252 matching files and 66 apparent gaps — but 64 were .gdoc/.gsheet pointer stubs, which Drive holds natively. Only two were real, and Lane B was never as complete as last session assumed.

05

Your one manual step

  1. Open Photos → Albums → ⌫ purge queue once the chain stages it.
  2. Scroll it. It is a review surface, not death row — favorite anything you want rescued.
  3. Press ⌘A then ⌘⌫.
  4. Recently Deleted → Delete All to actually reclaim the bytes.
The key that matters

Inside an album, plain ⌫ only removes from the album. ⌘⌫ is Delete from Library. The wrong key means you believe you purged, and nothing happened — then the next cycle quietly re-queues it all.
