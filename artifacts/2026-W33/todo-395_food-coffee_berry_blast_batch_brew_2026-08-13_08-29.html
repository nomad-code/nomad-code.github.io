Disclosed processing

Process drives the cup

  1. Caturra and Colombia cherries are selected.
  2. Anaerobic honey fermentation is enriched with red fruit and Lactobacillus.
  3. Fermentation runs 48–72 hours in stainless-steel bioreactors.
  4. Coffee dries 3–6 days on patios and 2–5 days in mechanical silos.
  5. A 20-day stabilization follows in plastic bags lined with fique.
Sensory interpretation

Why “fake” is a reasonable descriptor

Fruit substrate plus a selected bacterial culture can create unusually concentrated fermentation-derived esters. Those aromatics may register as berry candy, cordial, yoghurt, or flavoring rather than subtle fruit character inherent to the seed and terroir. This does not prove syrup was applied after roasting; it means the flavor is materially process-driven.

BPO recommendation

Make the intervention legible

  • Disclose “co-fermented with red fruit and Lactobacillus” at point of sale.
  • Describe the batch brew as overtly process-forward or candy-like.
  • Offer a conventional washed coffee beside it.
  • Review extraction and holding time; cooling can amplify fermented-fruit aromatics.
