Ten real cafés, one inflated scale

The audit brief: is this Bratislava top-ten honest, and how do the numbers hold up next to Europe's actual GOAT rooms? Roster verdict — 10/10 exist and are open, including the two that smelled fabricated (Temný OST BLOCK and Pán Králiček Priestor are both real). Score verdict — everything is inflated by roughly a point. A 9.8 for Goriffee puts a Košická-street café at Tim Wendelboe altitude; nothing in the international record supports that.

Four structural findings. (1) Inflation ≈ 1.0–1.2 across the board — the original scale left no room above Bratislava for Oslo or Copenhagen. (2) Compression — a 1.3-point spread said the city's 10th-best café is world elite; the honest spread is wider. (3) Double-count — OST BLOCK and Temný OST BLOCK are branches of one operation and should be one line. (4) Omission — Concept Coffee Roasters, a peer-adopted own-roast program on the same street as Goriffee, wasn't on the board at all; Košická is the city's roaster row.
Where Bratislava actually lands: a strong European second tier. black., Goriffee and Sweet Beans roast at a level worth a detour; Brew Bar pours GOAT-tier beans on guest days. Nobody in the city is yet buying green or placing on international stages the way the anchor six do — high-8s is a compliment, not a demotion.
