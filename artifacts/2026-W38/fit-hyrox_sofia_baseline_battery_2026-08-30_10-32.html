what the 67:59 actually is

Framing

Tenerife is not a PB race. It's experimental — Mexico City is the PB race, doubles isn't optimised for. So a good Tenerife is a clean read on every chunk, not a number. Cap the HR, hold the plan, come home with data.

worth checking before Oct

The calendar has Mexico City Oct 30 as Singles Pro. A Pro PB isn't comparable to an Open PB — different loads, different sport. Your open PB is 1:08:43 at Bangkok. If the plan is to beat that number, MEX has to be Open.

The battery

All doable in the Sofia gym. Each is 15–25 minutes of work, done fresh. Run every transition — walking one corrupts the roxzone read.

T1

Compromised km

~25 min · resolves the fork · do this first
4 × [ 1 km run  +  1 station ]

    stations   ski 1000m, row 1000m,
               ski 1000m, row 1000m
    no rest run → station
    no rest between rounds
    cap HR at 159 on the runs

159 is your lab LTP. Above it the lactate slope is steep and it stops being a race simulation.

readout — mean pace of runs 2–4

Run 1 is always fast and always lies.

T2

Opener

chunk A · ~15 min · the one you already liked
1 km run @ goal pace
  → straight into 1000m SkiErg
readout — ski split + HR at the line

Bangkok's ski was 4:20 off a 5:59 opener. Sofia on 28 Aug ran 2045 m at 2:12/500m — twice race distance at race pace — so the ski isn't the problem. This checks it holds with a kilometre in front of it.

T3

Sled repeat

chunk B · ~20 min · 23% of the race
4 × [ 50m sled push + 50m sled pull ]

    90 s easy jog between rounds
    race loads, open division

Squat went 90→100 kg × 6, deadlift 125→130 × 5. Strength went up. This asks whether repeatability did.

T4

Grind

chunk C · ~20 min · most invariant chunk, 25.4%
80m burpee broad jump
  → 1 km run
  → 1000m row
  continuous, no stops
readout — HR at three points

End of BBJ, end of run, end of row. The drift matters more than the time. This is where the Close gets bought — if HR is pinned here, T5 is decided before you start it.

T5

The Close

chunk E · ~18 min · your chunk
100m sandbag lunge
  → 1 km run
  → 100 wall balls
readout — the break pattern, not the time

Wall balls are bimodal: unbroken ≈ 4:05–4:45, broken ≈ 5:00–5:45. The gap is bigger than your whole farmers carry. Record where the first break lands and how many you got before it.

Bangkok: 100 unbroken in 4:08 off a run 8 at 156.7 bpm. Yokohama: 11:38. Same athlete, fourteen months apart — the widest range of anything you do.

T0

Six-minute max

not a chunk · the ceiling · costs a day
6 min all-out, treadmill @ 1% grade
readout — distance → vVO₂max · peak HR → is 166 still true

The Oct 2025 lab is ten months old and was probably a fitness low. Every band on this page is anchored to it. One six-minute effort re-anchors the whole model. Do it last.
