Division is the second partition key

A pooled table carried a filtered statistic, and nobody reading it could tell. Fixing it meant going back to the portal — which held fourteen roxzone measurements per race the repo had been deriving by subtraction, and three races the repo had never seen at all.

The catch

The table said n=6. The number was n=3.

CHUNKS.md printed six races in one table with a stdev, open singles row beneath it. The code was right — model.py:150 filters division == "HYROX". But only three of those six rows are open singles.

what was pooled

PRO Osaka and Yokohama carry heavier sled, farmers, sandbag and a heavier wall ball to a higher target. DOUBLES Taipei splits every station between two athletes, and its age group is the team's — 35-39, against 30-34 on every singles record.

Reading those six rows as one athlete is reading four years of ageing and three load standards as a single trend.

The rule now encoded: division partitions the data before any statistic, exactly as venue does. Authority is the event-id prefix — H_, HPRO_, HE_, HD_, HDP_, HMR_ — never a row label, which the portal often leaves blank. A pooled table may never carry a filtered statistic: annotate division and n, or split it. Prefer splitting.

Recovery

Why the sweep had never worked

The repo's events.json held 344 display names — "2026 Bangkok" — where the portal wants H_BKK26. The real ids are on the season start page, about 2,400 of them. Once you have those, a name search is one request and 400 ms.

  1. Harvested 2,126 real event ids across all nine seasons.
  2. Name-swept every one. The portal 403s after roughly 1,400 requests, which the first pass recorded as 689 clean misses — all of season-8 and season-9, exactly where the races are. Added backoff and retried to zero errors.
  3. Pulled each detail page and identity-checked it against nationality and age group, writing rejects with reasons rather than dropping them silently.
  4. Rebuilt durations from timing-mat crossings. Every race reconciles to adjustment 0.
the payoff

A detail row's seconds field is time of day, not a duration — the race clock is in cumulative_seconds. Diffing consecutive crossings yields 14 roxzone rows per race, where the hand-transcribed files had none. Roxzone is now observed everywhere, not derived by subtraction.

The missing races

They were all doubles, and doubles cannot be found by name

On HD_, HDP_ and HMR_ events the ranking list is keyed by team. search[name] matches the team; search[lastname], search[member] and search[team_member] are silently ignored — the portal returns the unfiltered first page and echoes the parameter into all 100 row hrefs, which greps as 100 matches. That is why doubles never surfaced in eight seasons of sweeping.

Member names are rendered, inside an <h4 class="…type-relay_member"> — not the <div> a first pass assumes, which is its own silent zero. Walking 150 venue aggregates plus every season-9 doubles day found five races, three of them absent from the repo entirely.

completeness is not claimed

Seasons 1–5 and non-aggregate day events outside season-9 were not walked. A doubles race missing from this history is not evidence it did not happen — the gap stays typed rather than closed by silence.
