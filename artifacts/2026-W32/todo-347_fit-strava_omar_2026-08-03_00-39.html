Where Apple Health helps

A fresh export is worth doing. It strengthens context and joins; it does not magically observe the event details the watch never captured.

It can add

  • body mass on test and race dates
  • continuous HR, resting HR, HRV and recovery context
  • sleep context and cardio-fitness observations where recorded
  • workout and route cross-checks against Strava

It cannot add

  • barbell load, reps, RIR or failed reps
  • valid pull-up, push-up, squat or wall-ball standards
  • sled surface and venue friction
  • HYROX roxzone discipline or Murph partition strategy
