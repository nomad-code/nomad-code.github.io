K-1 tax flow

Partner, not W-2 — so premiums are visible income that mostly washes out. The wash has exactly one leak, and it's small.

1
Rev. Proc. 93-27 profits interest → partnerNo §106 exclusion. The W-2 "premiums are invisible" treatment does not apply.
2
Found-paid premiums → K-1 guaranteed paymentsOrdinary income to Brendan, deductible to Found.
3
SEHI deduction — Form 7206 → Schedule 1100% of medical + dental premiums deducted → income-tax-side wash.
4
The leak: SEHI never reduces SE incomeDraws ≈ $200k sit at/above the Social Security wage base, so the marginal hit is Medicare-side only: 2.9% + 0.9% Additional Medicare ≈ $300–450/yr on a ~$10.5–13k premium.

Benefit map

The PPO's real value is catastrophic US protection plus ACA preventive at 100% while stateside. Abroad it's an accumulator-crediting machine, not a payer.

Medical · in-network

  • Deductible $1,500 → OOP max $3,000 (strong Platinum cap)
  • ACA preventive list at 100%, no deductible
  • To map from SBC: copays, imaging/lab cost-share, prior-auth list, telehealth

Medical · out-of-network + abroad

  • Deductible $3,000 → OOP max $6,000
  • BCBS Global Core = pay-and-claim; likely adjudicated OON → small bills reimburse ≈ $0
  • File anyway — every EOB credits the accumulator for a same-year big event
  • Verify: emergency abroad sometimes pays at in-network level — changes everything

Dental · Principal PPO

  • Preventive in-network 100% — the reliably harvestable value (~$400–600/yr of cleanings for $0, timed to US trips)
  • ~$1,500 annual carrier max
  • Verify: deductible, waiting periods on majors, frequency limits, missing-tooth clause, pre-treatment estimates

The nomad gap

Care calendar: cluster ACA preventive + 2× dental cleanings into US trips; age-triggered screenings per USPSTF; opportunistic commodity care abroad when a US window is missed.

Guardrail — value, not consumption.

The optimization target is greatest evidence-based health value, never spending to a deductible. No low-value testing to "use" benefits: covered status, medical necessity, radiation, false positives, and downstream cascades all count against a claim's real value.
