practice · dha world

portal-v2

A monorepo that already ships. Bun workspaces (not pnpm), mise toolchain, Turborepo as a thin task/cache layer — no ^build, virtual ci:run, the one cross-package edge wired outside turbo on purpose.

Its quiet strengths: continuous legacy-parity harnesses in tools/, layered SOPS/age secrets per host, one-command onboarding, remote turbo cache in a single CI job.

Role here: inspiration and existence proof — never referenced by name in company contexts.

pressure · found world

platform

One shell, achieved (TECH-728 collapsed four variants). Client bundle contract done, every data path slug-parameterized, fail-closed.

The debts: client slug baked into the image and re-read six times; manifest tabs read by no UI; upstream chat engine imported by hand-maintained snapshot ledger; client data synced across repos by script.

Role here: the sandbox — the first real app to live on the spine, and the source of the "many clients, one shell" requirement.

Role here: the direction of travel. Implementation starts with the TECH-729 prototype ladder.

the one open decision

  • Runtime client resolution vs per-service isolation. The harness dream (one deployed shell, client resolved at runtime) contradicts platform's written security thesis (isolation as IAM policy, one service per client). Middle path: keep one lane per client, resolve the slug from lane env at runtime — kills the build-time bake, keeps the isolation story intact, and leaves the door open to a true multi-client shell later. This is the question to align with Rob on before the packages split, because it decides what "app" means in apps/.
