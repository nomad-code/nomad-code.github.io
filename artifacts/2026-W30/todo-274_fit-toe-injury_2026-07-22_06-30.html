Ceiling: about 3–5 km easy until swelling clearly trends down. No sand, barefoot running, speed work, jumping, or back-to-back escalations without a morning check.

Management

Buddy tape

Pad between fourth and fifth toes; two snug—not tight—strips at base and tip of toe segments. Avoid bulky pressure over the MTP joint. Retape after sweat or shower; remove overnight for an untaped morning check.

Shoes + swelling

Use a stiff-soled or well-cushioned shoe with a roomy toe box. Elevate above heart in 20–30 minute blocks; ice 15–20 minutes after an incident. Prefer walking or sitting with the foot propped over long static standing.
