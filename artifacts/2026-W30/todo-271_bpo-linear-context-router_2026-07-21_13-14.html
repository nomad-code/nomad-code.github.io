The operating grammar

1

Team

A durable domain that earns a permanent prefix: GOLF, CAFE, FIT, TRVL. The primary identity-bearing edge.

2

Initiative

A high rock: a long-horizon objective coordinating several finishable projects.

3

Project

A bounded outcome with a real ending. Continuous categories should not hide here forever.

4

Label

Controlled, orthogonal metadata. Place belongs here—place:japan or place:bali—until the place becomes an operating domain.
