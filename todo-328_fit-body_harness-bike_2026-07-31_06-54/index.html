Fracture mode: push the engine, protect the toe

Use seated BikeErg efforts with a roomy, stiff-soled shoe. Begin with the 4K, 10K, and 20-minute tests only if the toe remains quiet. No standing sprints, high-torque climbing, technical riding, or forced toe-off until clinical clearance.
