the answer

Venue-first, account-carried

01

The question was "can I do it everywhere?" — and the honest answer is yes, if you flip the model: don't carry hardware to the world, carry an identity through the world's hardware. Three networks cover your entire calendar.

passport nº1 · asia + growing

Golfzon Global

Free account, PIN-code login at any networked Golfzon venue outside Korea/China. Your rounds, handicap and swing videos (nasmo) follow you from Fukuoka's Nakasu golf bars to Golfzon's own Tokyo range to Taipei's mega-clubs — and free networked tournaments (Golfzon Live Festival) run on rented bay time. Set it up before Fukuoka — email signup verified sufficient, no local phone number needed.

passport nº2 · europe + us

Trackman account

Free app profile; QR quick-login at bays running Trackman's TPS software and scores post to global monthly tournaments (free entry). Copenhagen is Trackman's home turf, PINPIN Oslo and every Five Iron run it, and the NEXT Golf Tour ($130 qualifiers, ~$1M 2026 purses, DP World Tour starts) is there if your game ever gets scary-good.

us city layer

Five Iron app

Free account; real-money on-demand tournaments with live leaderboards across locations, free Callaway rentals everywhere. Covers DC, Boston, Chicago, Miami and (via Fort Worth) Dallas on your route — one profile, every venue.

the rapsodo question

Should you carry a launch monitor?

05

Short answer: not yet. The physics is against hotel rooms, and you don't mind house clubs — which removes the only strong argument for hauling gear.

the physics

Hotel rooms don't swing

Every monitor wants a 9–10 ft ceiling; hotels give you ~8. Radar units (Rapsodo, Garmin, Mevo) also need 14–16 ft of depth. And TSA bans any golf club from carry-on — the kit means a checked bag, every flight, forever.

the math

Venues are cheaper than gear

A full travel kit (monitor, net, mat, clubs) runs 12–18 kg and $1,200+. That buys 20–40 hours of Trackman-class bays that come with screens, clubs and air-conditioning — roughly a season of play on your route.

if you ever buy

Square Golf, $699

The one travel-shaped unit: 470 g, no subscription, camera sits beside the ball so no radar depth, GSPro-official. Buy it the day you commit to SGT + a gaming laptop + month-long stays with a garage or 9 ft living room (Richmond fits). Until then: venues.
