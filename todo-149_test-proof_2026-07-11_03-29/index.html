03the seam that made it testable

The legend items were click-only divs — invisible to keyboards, screen readers, and test runners alike. Two attributes fixed all three at once (same pattern as the nearby-card review fix).

aria-pressed on the legend

Toggle state machines can assert, keyboards can reach.

<div className={styles.legendItemContainer}
+  role="button" tabIndex={0}
+  aria-pressed={visible}
+  onKeyDown={/* Enter · Space */}
   onClick={() => toggleCategory(id)}>

data-category-id on every pin

Per-category marker counts become observable facts.

<div className={styles.mapMarker}
   style={markerStyle}
+  data-category-id={categoryId}
>
