01 / what happened

The data was never on the Mac. It was on the phone's screen.

You wanted your 5-iron swing stats out of GolfJoy as a file you own. The honest finding: GolfJoy keeps nothing on this Mac — the iOS-on-Mac app containers are empty stubs, there's no local iPhone backup, and the "GolfJoy Space" cloud API is unproven. Every shot lives inside GolfJoyPro on the iPhone. But you'd already screenshotted the Practice data (5i) list — so OCR was the right call, and it turned out to be the complete one.

how the 6 screenshots fit together One wide, scrollable table captured as 3 column-blocks × 2 row-ranges (top rows 1–11, bottom rows 3–19). Rows 3–11 appear in both halves of every block — a built-in overlap check. Then an independent macOS Vision OCR pass read all six again. Manual read, overlap, and Vision all agreed on every cell. Nothing was guessed.

04 / going forward

no more OCR GolfJoy was a one-time rescue. From here, capture cleanly with swingnerds.com + GSPro — both export real CSVs that drop straight into ~/ark/golf/spica/. The phone-extraction paths (Mac-app sync, iPhone backup, cloud API) are retired. Toolkit lives in bpo/projects/golf-spica/.

This CSV is the sim/launch-monitor half of the golf corpus — complementary to the 4.7 GB TrackMan range data mapped in dna/golf.md. Same club, indoors vs the range, ready to join into one lifetime shot table.
